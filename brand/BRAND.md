# Vantage — brand kit

Vantage is the research desk: concalls, results, sectors, ideas and Bytes, for Indian listed companies.
Idea: a vantage point. The mark is a V meeting a horizon line, with the sun just above it.

## Files
- `vantage-mark.svg` — the square mark (app icon, favicon, avatar)
- `vantage-logo.svg` — mark + wordmark (headers, Bytes cards)

## Type
- Headlines: **Bricolage Grotesque** 600–800 (Google Fonts)
- Body: **Public Sans** 400–700
- Numbers: **IBM Plex Mono** 500–600

## Bytes cards (1200 × 675, exported at 2×)
- Top bar: logo left; series ("Sector pattern", "Thematic", "Company") and date right.
- Kicker in saffron caps, one-line headline in ink with the key phrase in forest, one-line dek.
- Left: one chart (bars or diverging bars). Right: up to three stat tiles.
- Bottom: the **Vantage read** band — ink background, paper text, one sentence.
- Footer: sources, "For information only. Not investment advice."
- Our own data first (calls, results, sector tracker); any outside figure is credited by name.
- No company logos, no stock photos, no copying another firm's layout or wording.
