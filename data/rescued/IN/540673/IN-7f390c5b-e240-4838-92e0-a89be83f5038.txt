Dhiraj Singh: Thank you. Good afternoon, everyone, and welcome to the Q2 FY27 earnings call. Before we 
begin, it is with deep sadness that I share the passing of our Founder Chairman, Shri Ravindra 
Kishore Sinha Ji on the 30 th of October. He was the visionary who built this Company and his 
leadership, values and guidance have shaped everything we are today. His loss is deeply felt by 
all of us at SIS. On behalf of the Board, the management team and all our employees, I extend 
our heartfelt condolences to his family. 
We will carry forward the legacy he leaves behind and remain committed to the values he stood 
for. It is with that spirit and commitment that we continue our journey. And I would now like to 
take you through our performance for the quarter. I hope you have had a chance to look at our 
results and the earnings note, which was uploaded on the stock exchanges and the Company 
website yesterday. 
In Q1, we had called FY27 an inflection year. Q2 establishes the trend. This was a milestone 
quarter for SIS on every measure that matters: record revenue, record EBITDA, record profit 
and margin expansion across all 3 of our business segments. 
Consolidated revenue for the quarter was a record INR 4,816 crore, up 28.1% year-on-year basis 
and 4.6% sequentially. Even excluding APS, on an organic basis, the growth has been 18.6% 
year-on-year and 4.4% quarter -on-quarter. Please note, this is our ninth consecutive quarter of 
sequential revenue growth. 
 At the same time, EBITDA was a record INR 222 crore also, up 32.7% year-on-year and 7.8% 
sequentially. This is the fifth record EBITDA quarter in a row. Even without APS, this has been 
a growth in EBITDA of 23.8% year -on-year and 6.8% quarter -on-quarter basis. Margins also 
expanded and came in at 4.6%, which is up 16 basis points year-on-year and our best in the past 
6 quarters. Profit after tax was INR 111 crore, up 37.1% year -on-year and the highest in the 
Company's history. 
 I want to draw your attention to one point in particular. Both EBITDA growth of 32.7% and 
PAT growth of 37% ran ahead of revenue growth of 28.1%. So it means we are not simply 
getting bigger. We are getting better also as we get bigger. This is the operati ng leverage we 
have been building forward. 
 On Scale, trailing 12 -month revenues now stands at INR 18,094 crore and our annualized run 
rate is now nearing the INR 20,000 crore mark, and we are at INR 19,300 crore. For the half 
year, revenue was up to INR 9,420 crore, which is up almost 29%, and it is already 59% of our 
entire FY26 revenue.

H1 EBITDA was at INR 430 crore, up 34.4%, with margins up 19 basis points and H1 profit 
was INR 212 crore, which is up 22.3%. Let me now spend a moment on the labor codes because 
this is central to our outlook. The codes are a clear positive for SIS, and our early readiness is 
now visible in the numbers, too. 
 We began our internal compliance program on the June 1, and India EBITDA has grown 13.8% 
over the 6 months since the codes were introduced, with the India margins improving from 5.2% 
in Q4 FY26 to 5.4% this quarter. 
Higher wage and statutory costs are being fully passed through to customers, lifting both the 
revenue and the margin. Enforcement, though, is State by State and still early. Karnataka, 
Haryana, Uttar Pradesh and Uttarakhand has revised the minimum wages. Others are expected 
to follow as state model rules are notified. The major part of the repricing benefit is, therefore, 
still ahead of us. Over time, the codes will also create a level playing field between organized 
and unorganized providers, which we expect to be accretive at both the EBITDA and PAT levels. 
 Now turning to the various business segments. Security Solutions India delivered a record 
revenue of INR 2,144 crore, which is up 38.9% year -on-year and 7% sequentially. This is the 
10th consecutive quarter of sequential growth. Even without APS, the organic growth for 
Security Solutions India was 15.7% year-on-year and 6.8% quarter-on-quarter. EBITDA was a 
record INR 113 crore, which is up 37.2% year-on-year and 10.4% sequentially. Without APS, 
it is 18.9% year-on-year and 8.6% quarter-on-quarter basis. Also the fifth record quarter in a row 
now. Margins improved 16 basis points in the segment sequentially to 5.3% now and are broadly 
flat year-on-year basis. 
On the International front, Security Solutions International recorded its highest ever revenue at 
INR 2,020 crore, up 25.7% year-on-year and 6.3% on a constant currency basis. EBITDA was 
INR 72 crore, which is up 35%, with the margins improving 23 basis points year -on-year to 
3.6%. Following the Fair Work Commission's 4.7% minimum wage increase in Australia, price 
revisions under a number of client contracts are tied to contract anniversaries, which creates a 
temporary lag between cost and price pass-through, but will happen in due course. 
Facility Management Solutions posted a record revenue of INR 677 crore and a record EBITDA 
of INR 38 crore, its ninth consecutive record EBITDA quarter. The margin expansion continues. 
It is now at 5.7%, and is the highest in the past 6 years, up almost 50 basis points year-on-year. 
On our return ratios, the return on capital employed stands at 16.5%, up from 14.3% a year ago, 
and the return on equity is also at 16%. 
 During this quarter, we also completed our fifth buyback. SIS was among the first in the market 
to announce an open market buyback, and we completed it in 45 trading sessions, acquiring 
approximately 25 lakh shares for INR 106 crore. With this, the total capital returned to 
shareholders since listing now stands at a little over INR 700 crore. 
Let me address also the working capital, which was the one soft spot in this quarter. Operating 
cash flow was negative INR 99 crore on a working capital outflow of INR 273 crore, of which 
INR 216 crore sat in our international business due to delayed collections from one of the largest

client, the defense segment. This is a timing issue, not a credit issue, and the payments, in fact, 
have already been received in October. 
Net debt to EBITDA moved to 1.24x from 1.05x at the end of Q1, primarily due to the working 
capital outflow in Security Solutions International and a bit because of the buyback payouts of 
INR 92 crore. 
However, gross debt was broadly flat at about INR 2,047 crore. On the DSO front, India security

from 73 days, which is better both sequentially and also on a year-on-year basis. FM was stable 
at 93 days. The increase was largely concentrated in international, as I mentioned, which is 
caused due to a temporary timing difference and will be normalized through this quarter. 
To summarize, this has been a quarter of record revenue, record EBITDA, our highest ever 
quarterly profit, profit growing faster than revenue, margin expansion in every segment, both 
ROCE and ROE above 16% and a structural tailwind from the labor codes whe re most of the 
benefit is still to come. We remain focused on margin expansion and on bringing working capital 
back in line.
