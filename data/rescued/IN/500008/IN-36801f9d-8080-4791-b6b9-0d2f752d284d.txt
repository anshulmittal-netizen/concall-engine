2026, we have achieved a revenue of around INR4,108 crores on consolidated basis. With about 
94% of the revenue coming from lead-acid business, which grew by around 18%. 
The new energy business grew by more than 30%, recording a revenue of around INR232 crores. 
Our revenue growth has remained strong, driven by sustained volume momentum in both 
aftermarket and OEM segments. We continue to strengthen and deepen our market position in 
automotive and home energy sectors. 
OEM segment continued to retain the volume growth momentum of around 17% in four-wheeler 
and more than 30% in two-wheeler on Y-o-Y basis. Domestic aftermarket volumes reflected a 
growth of around 6% in both four-wheeler and two-wheeler on Y-o-Y basis. Further, we also 
delivered growth of more than 50% in home energy business, both on tubular batteries and home 
UPS. 
The trading revenue in the current quarter moderated to 10% of the lead-acid battery revenue. 
On the international revenues, we improved on a quarter-on-quarter basis, but on a Y-o-Y basis, 
we de-grew by around 20% owing to the volume drop in Middle East markets. We are expecting 
to recover these volumes in these markets in the subsequent quarters. 
Lead-acid telecom volumes witnessed a one-time spike during the quarter of more than 30%, 
and lithium-ion telecom packs grew by around 15% on Y-o-Y basis. This one-off uptick in lead-
acid volumes is due to delays in yearly lithium telecom pack volumes negotiations with the 
OEMs.

During the quarter, on a combined basis, we continue to maintain a market share of over 60%, 
and meanwhile, UPS volumes sustained modest volume growth of around 8%. New Energy 
business during the quarter continued to deliver robust performance with revenue supported by 
increased demand for telecom pack volumes, which grew by around 15% during the quarter on 
Y-o-Y basis. However, EV volumes faced some demand headwinds during the quarter, which 
is expected to recover in the coming months. 
Further, we are happy to share that our ePositive Lab commenced operations on October 26, 
which will support the new energy business in its technology advancements. The board also 
approved our next phase of investment in prismatic cell gigafactory and pack assembly capacity 
with a total capex outlay of around INR2,320 crores, which is expected to be completed within 
next 30 months.

would initially cater to stationary applications and later on for mobility applications. The pack 
capacity enhancement is done to cater to new pack opportunities which we are foreseeing for 
various storage & mobility applications. 
The margins during Q1 on standalone EBITDA is around 10.1%. The margins are stable on 
quarter-on-quarter basis, but we were impacted on Y-o-Y basis. This is primarily due to elevated 
material cost and increased spending on our strategic initiatives, namely Amaron Assist and the 
Factory of the Future, and also a higher OEM mix contributed to the margin moderation to some 
extent. 
As communicated in our earlier calls, we have launched Amaron Assist during Q1 FY27 in 
Hyderabad, a project which is focused on providing automotive services in B2C segment. Based 
on the pilot response and our future plans, Board has approved setting up a wholly owned 
subsidiary under the brand name Amaron Assist for carrying out this business. 
We'll be scaling up this project to new locations gradually over the near future. This business 
would be having some cash burn in the initial period, which also had some impact in the Q2 
margins. Further, we also increased our spending on improving our manufacturing excellence 
by way of capacity enhancement in our existing automotive plants, which also added to the 
moderation of margins. 
Higher expenses on these strategic initiatives during the quarter led to margin moderation of 
around 1%. The other major aspect that led to the moderation in profitability is a significant 
increase in the procurement costs of alloys, sulfuric acid and poly. We took price hikes during 
Q1 FY27 to offset these cost pressures. However, acid and poly have further increased during 
Q2. 
To absorb these costs, we again took a price increase of around 2% during Q2 FY27, which 
should help offset a portion of these cost pressures. Along with this Q2 price hike, till H1 of the 
current financial year, our cumulative price hike was around 5%. At a consolidated level, the 
margins were lowered by around 1.3% to 8.8% due to additional cost at our new energy business 
towards the product development and other fixed costs pertaining to our Giga-1 and BESS plans.
