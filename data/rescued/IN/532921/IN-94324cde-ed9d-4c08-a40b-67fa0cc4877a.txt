Ashwani Gupta: Thank you, Rahul. Hello everyone. Thank you for joining our 
conference call to discuss the recent Evos transaction. APSEZ agrees to acquire 100% 
in Evos BV from Igneo Infrastructure Partners for an enterprise value of 2.56 billion 
euros. The equity value of the transaction is at 1.18 billion euros, implying a multiple of 
11.7x of CY25 EV by EBITDA. Evos is a highly profitable business with 392 million euros 
in revenue and an EBITDA margin of 56% in CY25. The regulatory approval process and 
the works council consultation process will start immediately. This is an all -cash 
transaction that will be funded via debt, and on a pro -forma basis, we expect APSEZ's 
net debt to EBITDA to be at 2. 76x but revert to our guidance of 2.5x net debt to EBITDA 
by the end of FY27. 
Evos is an extremely strategic asset for APSEZ. Evos is the second largest independent 
liquid storage operator in Europe with eight port -based terminals in five countries: 
Netherlands, Germany, Spain, Belgium, and Malta. These terminals, with a capacity of 
6.3 million cubic meters, are in the heart of most important energy and industrial hubs 
in Europe, including the ARA region. These terminals store, transfer, blend, and deliver 
other value-added services for a variety of traditional and transition sustainable fuels. 
Importantly, while traditional fuels has been always Evos' stronghold, it is also an early 
mover in the transition fuel storage space. Evos has strong capabilities across many 
new-age liquids, including SAF , biofuels, methanol, bioethanol, etc., and is set to play a 
crucial role in Europe's energy transition trajectory. Evos ' value proposition is further 
strengthened by excellent pipeline connectivity to customers and various pipeline 
networks. 
Why we acquired Evos: 
Number one reason is the the macro. Evos operates in a stable macro and currency 
environment, offering strong top-down stability to its overall operations. Moreover, Evos 
operates in regions that are at the heart of the world's liquid storage and energy hubs. 
Another reason is Evos' core competency. Evos' extensive liquid storage portfolio across 
traditional and transition fuels offers high revenue and operating cash visibility. Evos is 
also the preferred storage partner for many tier-1 blue-chip customers. 
APSEZ has access to local funding, including foreign currency bank facilities. 
Another reason is Evos' competent leadership team. We are committed to ensuring the 
management continuity post -acquisition, retaining the leadership and expertise that 
have been instrumental in Evos' success and will be instrumental in taking it to the next 
level. 
Evos ticks all the boxes which I always explain in our analyst call and whenever I meet 
you all, of our international M&A strategy. 
Evos will continue to contribute immediately to our top line and bottom line without 
diluting APSEZ's EBITDA profile. During our Ambition 2031 time frame, the ROCE of the 
asset will be increased to double digits, in line with APSEZ's overall ROCE profile driven 
by revenue growth, operating efficiency, and efficient capex deployment. Pro -forma 
consolidated debt for the transaction will also be within our guided 2.5x net debt to 
EBITDA. Last but not least, sustainability is a core component of Evos ' profile, and Evos' 
advanced sustainability credentials will further enhance APSEZ's overall ESG profile 
across various environmental and social KPIs. 
Now let me share with you how Evos will contribute to APSEZ moving forward. 
First, Evos will translate into significant upside in the future driven by price realization, 
export potential of key liquids including diesel, and transition to high-value liquids 
including methanol, bio methanol, SAF , etc. APSEZ can also use this platform to expand 
its presence in energy transition within Europe and beyond Europe around the world. 
Evos will add sizable euro-denominated hard currency earnings from A - rated sovereign 
family in Europe, that will enhance overall APSEZ's risk profile. Evos will scale and 
expand APSEZ's liquid business to more than double the contribution as it is today from 
Mundra and Hazira terminals. That will give a very balanced mix of commodities 
between container, liquid, dry, and other commodities. Other benefits include access 
to cutting-edge technology and a great engineering talent pool within Evos with strong 
innovation and R&D capabilities that will also be needed in India moving forward, 
following the energy transition in the near future . Moreover, India -Europe FTA will also 
accelerate further business opportunities. We are delighted to welcome Evos to the 
APSEZ family. We are very excited to partner with the Evos team as we integrate our 
operations and execute the next phase in its corporate journey. I would like to thank you 
for having the strong confidence in APSEZ and for your patience, and now I will answer 
your queries. Thank you.
