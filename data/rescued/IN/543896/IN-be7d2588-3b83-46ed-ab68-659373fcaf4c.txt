 Second, on our joint venture with Zollner Elektronik AG. Zollner is one of Europe’s leading 
EMS companies, with more than six decades of experience and over 12,500 employees across 
26 locations worldwide. The joint venture, Zollner Avalon Private Limited, will focus on PCB 
assemblies, box builds and integrated systems in India. Avalon can supply the JV with 
components such as metal parts, plastics and cable harnesses. It will initially serve the India 
operations of Zollner’s established global customers, in rail, industrial, test and measurement, 
medical and life sciences and other sectors. Medical & life sciences and test & measurement will 
be new verticals for Avalon. 
 At the start, Zollner will hold 51% and Avalon 49%. This lets Zollner lead the early operations, 
drawing on its customer relationships and engineering expertise. Three years after the joint 
venture starts commercial production, Avalon will have an option, subject to certain conditions, 
to buy 2% and become the majority shareholder at 51%. So, the structure gives Avalon a clear 
path to majority as the business matures. 
 The Zollner partnership is a strategic accelerator for Avalon. For Zollner’s customers, it offers a 
ready manufacturing base in India. For Avalon, it gives access to Zollner’s global customer 
relationships and expands our presence in high-complexity manufacturing. It is a natural 
extension of our strategy: high-mix, high-complexity products, long product life cycles and long-
term customer relationships. Until the JV’s own facility is ready, the joint venture will outsource 
production for its new customers to Avalon. 
 Our relationship with Zollner goes beyond the joint venture. To strengthen this further, our
