 As a strategic initiative, on 6th October 2026, we signed a joint venture agreement with a 
company named Faurecia Automotive Seating India Limited, part of FORVIA, the global 
automotive technology group to establish Faurecia Anand Seating India Private Limited, which 
was subsequently incorporated on 19th October 2026. This represents another important step in 
Gabriel India's transformation into a diversified technology-led mobility solution enterprise. 
 The joint venture will focus on developing, manufacturing, supplying seat frames and complete 
seating systems for passenger vehicle, where Gabriel India will be holding 50% less 1 share and 
FORVIA will be holding 50% plus 1 share. 
 With this joint venture, we bring here FORVIA's global seating technology, product engineering, 
complete seat capabilities and manufacturing expertise with ANAND Group's strong 
relationship with Indian OEMs, extensive local operating footprint and deep unde rstanding of 
the domestic automotive market. 
 Importantly, this partnership builds on the trust ed relationship between FORVIA and ANAND 
Group in an emission control business that was going on before 2000. And with FORVIA, I 
think they acquired a partnershi p, this business globally sometime in 2009 -'10. So while the 
business of emission control has been before 2000, but partnership with FORVIA is almost 2009 
and 2010 onwards actually. 
 The venture is expected to benefit from access to FORVIA's wider seating products, engineering 
portfolio and to strengthen manufacturing supply chain and talent capabilities in India. The 
partnership is fully aligned with FORVIA's seating ambition to reach approximately 10% market 
share in India over the next 5 years as its first growth milestone. 
 For Gabriel India, entry into the passenger vehicle seating system is strategically important 
because it further broadens our product offering into a powertrain-agnostic category, strengthens 
our participation across the automotive value chain and provides a platform for long -term
