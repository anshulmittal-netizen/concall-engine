effect from 8th of July, we had undertaken business-level integration during this quarter. And 
as guided earlier, there were some transient impact on JB business top line performance during 
the quarter.

was largely flat year-on-year. JB India branded business grew at 7% for the quarter. 
International business was down by 7%. 
Torrent base business continued its strong growth momentum with revenues growing 18% for 
the quarter. The base India business, with 20% growth, continued to outperform IPM, while 
Brazil revenues were up 38%. On the generic side, US delivered strong 24% growth, while 
Germany revenues were down by 7%. The overall leverage, that is net debt to EBITDA, now 
stands at 1.8x.

Aman Mehta: Thanks, Sudhir. I will first talk about the JB integration update. Post the completion of merger

of divisions and field force in the India business.

integration activities, the India business of JB delivered robust underlying growth, which 
provides comfort in the long-term growth prospects of the business post-recovery. 
JB's international operations saw an impact from, again, the integration around plant network 
optimization and alignment of manufacturing and quality control systems. This led to 
postponement of deliveries in certain markets, and we see a gradual path to recovery in the 
subsequent quarters.
