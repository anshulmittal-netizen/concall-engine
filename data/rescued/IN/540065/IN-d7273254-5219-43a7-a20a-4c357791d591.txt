 In this backdrop, I will briefly touch upon our trends in the quarter. 
As you are aware, we have generated a large amount of liquidity on our balance sheet, between 
the capital raise and the deposits raised during the FCNR(B) scheme. For context, between both, 
we have added 35% of our balance sheet as of 31st May 2026. This liquidity is deployed as 
follows: 
a. First, prepaid or chosen not to renew the borrowings and bulk term deposits which were at 
higher cost. 
b. Grow our business across both wholesale and secured retail, where we have seen demand, 
and the business conditions continue to remain fairly stable. The wholesale lending is a 
highly rated corporates where we want to start or deepen the relationship. 
c. The balance liquidity is tactically deployed in a combination of the short-term investments 
and the short-term loans given to our wholesale customers. 
Given the large liquidity still on the balance sheet, we expect to continue this for the next few 
months. Over the medium term, we expect this liquidity to be fully deployed in the wholesale 
banking segment, where we want to use both our ability to underwrite larger exposures and the 
liquidity to strengthen the relationship with the better-rated corporates, and the secured retail 
through the branch banking route, again, to deepen the customer relationship. 
At the same time, while corporate deposits and borrowings have already seen reduction in the 
cost, we will continue to focus on building granular retail deposits and rationalized our deposit 
rates gradually. As you may have seen, we have already started on this journey across our 
savings and term deposits. We will continue our investments in upgrading our distribution across 
branches, people, and technology to deepen our network and improve the quality of our products 
and services.

with no disruptions so far. However, given deficient monsoons in certain districts, we continue 
to remain very watchful. We have also not seen any material impact on our portfolio arising 
from the conflict in the Middle East so far.

clients, where we are looking to deepen or start a new relationship.

deposit grew 24% Y-o-Y. This includes FCNR deposits raised by this quarter. As a result, our 
average CASA ratio stood at 24% as of 30th September. It would have been 28.1% ex-FCNR 
deposits.
