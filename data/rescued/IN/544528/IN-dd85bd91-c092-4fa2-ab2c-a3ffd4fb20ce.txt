Products, I welcome you to our Q2 Financial year '27 Earnings call and thank you for joining us 
today. The second quarter of financial year 2027 saw a sequential recovery in a challenging 
environment for the category, driven by continued progress in our value-added portfolio. 
Revenue from operations was INR2,267 million, up 20.3% quarter on quarter, though it was 
down 5% year-on-year, while operating EBITDA grew 23.6% and profit after tax grew 68.7% 
year-on-year. B2C revenue declined 2.5% year-on-year, with the pressure concentrated in the 
atta category where volumes declined following changes in the state government policy. 
In May, the West Bengal government decided to stop distributing atta through ration shops and 
started to supply wheat grain instead. Local flour millers who were earlier engaged in job work 
for the state government converting ration wheat into atta lost their business. They have since 
redirected their capacity towards selling atta in the unorganized segment, which has added to the 
competition in the open market. 
This affected the packaged category as a whole. As per NielsenIQ, the packaged atta category 
in West Bengal declined 6.3% year-on-year during the quarter and the broader packaged wheat-
based category covering Atta and maida suji declined 2.5% quarter from quarter. We believe the 
pressure on atta is temporary and expect the volumes to normalize in the medium term. 
Excluding Atta, B2C revenue grew 4.9% year-on-year and 26% quarter-on-quarter, which is a 
better reflection of our underlying demand for our brands. B2B revenue declined 13.8% year-
on-year, mainly due to lower service income and softer core product volumes. 
 E-commerce, including Quick Commerce, remains our fastest growing channel with a CAGR 
of over 53% between financial year '23 and financial year '26, and we are now expanding our e-
