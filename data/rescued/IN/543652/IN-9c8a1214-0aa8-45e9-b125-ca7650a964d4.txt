behaviour shifts. We are also evolving how we manage harder bucket collections.

 Until last quarter, external agencies handled roughly 30% of the hard bucket recoveries. We 
have now moved that capability fully in- house, combining AI with our own collections and 
branch teams. We thus run 2 models on hard bucket collections: One, dedicated collections team 
engaging customers directly towards resolution; and two, AI handling initial identification and 
contact before the branch teams take over. This transition hasn't cost us recovery performance.

engagement, less dependence on external agencies and a more scalable recovery model.

 Turning to how we are extracting more value from our distribution franchise we have already

seasonally soft month for mortgages, but May and June returned to Q4's average monthly levels. 
We continue to deliver on our right to win with no deviations on cash flows.

credit team. We will scale this gradually based on portfolio behaviour with potential for 
individual loans to reach around 10% of MFI disbursements over time. 
 Together, these initiatives let us extract more value from an existing distribution franchise, while

specific MFI customer segments where we can increase shar e of wallet, and we are developing
