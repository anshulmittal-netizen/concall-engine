with our press release. This has also been sent to the analysts who are there on our mailing list. The 
quarter was characterized by strong financial performance on back of improved r ealizations, even as 
geopolitical developments in the Hormuz region impacted global supply chains and led to delay s in the 
execution of certain strategic projects including DUDP. 
We reported standalone gross revenue of INR 43,857 crore, an increase of over 33% over the 
corresponding period of last year . Our average crude realization for the nomination fields during the 
quarter was USD 92.33 per barrel. Stand -alone profit after tax stood at INR 15,489 crore, registering a 
robust growth of over 57% YoYyear-on-year. Riding on the strong-earning momentum, the company also 
posted its highest ever H1 profit after tax of INR 32,522 crore. The board also declared its highest ever 
interim dividend of 160% that is INR 8 per share with total dividend outgo of INR 10,064 crore. 
 
 
5 
Despite a challenging cost environment marked by inflation and exchange rate volatility, ONGC 
maintained a strong focus on cost optimization during the quarter. The company's operating expenditure 
remained well controlled, reflecting the benefits of sustained operational efficiency initiatives and 
disciplined management of controllable costs across its operations. 
During HY FY’27, ONGC generated INR 7,050 crore of revenue from new-well gas, realizing an incremental 
INR 2,859 crore compared to the APM gas pricing regime, accounting for over 34% of company's 
nomination gas revenues. New-well gas continues to strengthen ONGC’s earnings profile. 
On the production front, ONGC’s oil and gas output remained broadly stable on a sequential basis during 
the quarter. Although it moderated marginally on a year-on-year basis, p. Production was impacted by 
supply- chain disruptions affecting certain projects, including DUDP as well as weather-related operational 
challenges in offshore areas. 
In addition, temporary customer offtake constraints, including a shutdown at the OTPC facility in Tripura 
affected gas production during the quarter. We are actively addressing these issues, with critical materials 
now being mobilized and additional wells expected to come on stream in the coming months. Accordingly, 
we expect a gradual improvement in productio n and are confident of recovering lost ground in the 
subsequent quarters. 
The TSP, that is Technical Services Provider Program at Mumbai High continues to deliver encouraging 
results, with green shoots visible and in MH Production, and demonstrating the positive impact of the 
interventions undertaken. Building on this success, ONGC is moving ahead with a larger drilling and 
intervention program aimed at further enhancing production from the field. 
The TSP program for the remaining Western Offshore assets, primarily the Neelam, and Bassein & Satellite 
fields, was awarded during the last quarter. Teams have been mobilized and detailed work plans are being 
finalized, with implementation expected to support production enhancement and other natural declines 
in these mature fields. 
ONGC reinforced its long -term growth pipeline during the quarter by awarding two major Western 
Offshore development projects with a combined investment of approximately INR 11,800 crore and an 
estimated hydrocarbon potential of 22 million metric ton of oil equivalent. The projects, covering MB-
OSN-2005/1, SD-4 and BS-17 and the additional development of Ratna-1 and NeelamLM-14 are expected 
to be completed over the next two to two- and- a- half years. 
On the exploration front, ONGC has renewed its focus on deepwater exploration under the Government 
of India's Samudra Manthan initiative. We are encouraged by the positive results from exploratory drilling 
in Mahanadi Offshore Basin, where gas has been encountered, affirming the prospectivity of the Indian 
Eastern Offshore basins. We are optimistic about the potential of emerging prospects in Utkal, Konark, 
and Mahanadi areas, which could open new growth avenues for the company. 
 
 
6 
A significant policy milestone during the quarter was issuance of detailed operational guidelines for the 
Samudra Manthan, National Offshore E xploration Scheme. To support this, ONGC is scaling up its 
deepwater drilling capabilities. One deepwater rig is already operational in Mahanadi Basin, while the 
second deepwater rig is expected to be mobilized during this financial year. 
We are also encouraged by the gas discoveries in the Silchar region. With the commissioning and 
expansion of IGGL gas pipeline network, these stranded gas resources are expected to be monetized, 
contributing to ONGC's future production growth. 
The ONGC Group posted a consolidated profit after tax of INR 18,535 crore in Q2 FY’27. Driven by solid 
contributions across its business, HSPCL rebounded strongly during the quarter on the back of improved 
GRMs, while ONGC Videsh and MRPL delivered notable performances. The results underscore the benefits 
of ONGC Group's integrated and diversified energy portfolio and its ability to deliver resilient earnings 
across business cycles. 
ONGC Petrochemicals Marketing Limited was incorporated on 7th October 2026 to serve as a single , 
integrated, market- facing platform for the marketing and trading of petrochemicals, chemicals, and 
related products across the ONGC’s Group. 
The initiative is aligned with ONGC's downstream and petrochemical strategy and is expected to enhance 
market reach and unlock greater value across the group's petrochemical value chain. ONGC is not only 
delivering robust earnings today, but it is also building the foundation for sustainable growth tomorrow 
through focused investment in production growth, portfolio diversification , and operational excellence. 
We are strengthening our ability to generate higher cash flows and enhance stakeholder returns. With
