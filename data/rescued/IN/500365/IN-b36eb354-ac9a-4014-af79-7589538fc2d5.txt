are due to be finalized this quarter or the next. Given the balance between consumption and 
capacity, we are confident that export markets will improve significantly and open up more 
value-added opportunities as conditions gradually stabilize.

India's only integrated manufacturer of extruded stainless steel Pipes and Tubes, we are well
