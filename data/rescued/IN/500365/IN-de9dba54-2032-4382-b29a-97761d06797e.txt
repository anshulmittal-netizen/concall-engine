headwind for exports.

gas, petrochemicals, fertilizers, thermal and nuclear power, defense, and infrastructure.

Make in India, the import substitution initiatives, we believe this opportunity will continue to 
expand over the coming years and remain central to our growth strategy alongside our export 
business.

healthy growth driven by strong domestic demand.
