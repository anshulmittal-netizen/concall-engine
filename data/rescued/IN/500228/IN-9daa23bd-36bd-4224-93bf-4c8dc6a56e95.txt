we are setting up processing centers across India. The first 0.5 million tonnes center at 
Khopoli, where we have commissioned the baler during the Quarter 2, and the shredder will be

 On the operating performance, despite a challenging geopolitical backdrop and the resulting 
pressures on supply chains and input costs, JSW Steel delivered a strong operational 
performance. Crude steel production and consolidated and India operations were the highest 
ever at 7.27 million and 7.07 million tonnes, respectively, both growing by about 5% YoY. 
 The BF-3 at Vijayanagar has ramped up well following light-up in the end of June, reaching 
about 90% utilisation during the quarter. The JVML operations achieved record quarterly 
production. However, our India operations achieved a utilisation of about 88%. 
 Our consolidated sales were the highest at 7.46 million tonnes, growing at 13% YoY. Indian 
operations sales were also the highest at 7.27 million tonnes, which grew by 14%, driven by a 
demand recovery in the second half of Q2. We achieved our highest ever domestic sales at 
6.29 million tonnes, up by 12%, outpacing the Indian market growth. 
 Our focus on value-added steels helped us to deliver record sales of value-added and special 
products of 4.11 million tonnes, up 11%. Our focus on strategic sectors continued. Institutional 
sales grew by 15% YoY. Auto sector sales were the highest ever, growing by 36% YoY. Sales 
to the appliance and renewable sectors also grew by a healthy 28% and 14%, respectively. 
 Coming to the financial results, in Quarter 2, JSW Steel reported consolidated revenue of 
Rs.54,486 crores and adjusted EBITDA of Rs.9,941 crores with an EBITDA margin of 18%. 
PAT stood at Rs.5,359 crores. The quarter was impacted by high cost and supply chain 
disruptions. 
 Overall input costs were higher, particularly coking coal, which increased by about $10 a 
tonne, a bit lower than our earlier guidance of $12 to $15 for this quarter. Iron ore costs were 
also slightly higher, among other input costs as well. Realisations fell quarter-on-quarter, 
mainly on account of Long steel prices. 
 Performance at our overseas operations improved sequentially, mainly driven by better 
realisations. Last quarter, we commissioned a vacuum tank degasser at Ohio. This will enable 
us to produce higher grade steel, especially API grades. Quarter 2 EBITDA at Ohio increased 
quarter-on-quarter, although operations were impacted by a scheduled maintenance shutdown 
in September.
