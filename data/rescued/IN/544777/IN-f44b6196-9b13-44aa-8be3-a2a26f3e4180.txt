tons per annum. This pan-India footprint enables us to operate close to major automotive and 
industrial clusters. During the year, we con tinue to scale operations and improve capacity

stronger, more capable, and more relevant to the evolving needs of our customers. 
 

But capacity alone does not create a sustainable competitive advantage. The real strength of 
our business lies in our integrated operations covering scrap procurement, segregation, 
processing, melting, alloy production, and customer delivery. We serve lead ing automotive 
OEMs and Tier 1 suppliers while expanding into industrial and emerging applications. Our 
diversified scrap sourcing network continues to support our operations. We work with a wide 
base of domestic and international suppliers while strengthe ning our local procurement 
capabilities. This gives us greater flexibility in securing raw materials and managing supply 
chain uncertainties. We are also developing a stronger capability in low -carbon and recycled 
aluminium. As customers place greater emphasis on reducing their environmental impact, our 
recycling-led model enables us to support their sustainability goals. 
 
We delivered a strong operating and financial performance in FY26. Total sales volume rose 
approximately 24% to 409,000 metric tonnes delivered, driven by capacity additions, improved 
utilization, and the stabilization of newer facilities. Aluminium sales volume grew by 
approximately 27%, while non -aluminium sales increased by approximately 18%. Revenue 
from operations rose about 29.5% to ₹8,640 crores in FY26. EBITDA increased by 50.42% to 
₹449.4 crores, with an EBITDA margin improving to approximately 5.2 % from 4.5% in the 
year FY25. Profit after tax grew by 47.3% to ₹228.4 crores, while the PAT margin increased to 
approximately 2.64%. This performance was supported by higher volumes, improved 
realization, operating leverage, a favorable product mix, and disciplined cost management. 
These gains also reflect our continued focus on technology and operational efficiency, which 
remain important to our growth plans and are helping us to improve productivity, strengthen 
processes, and enhance decision-making. We are using automation, ERP, and MIS to improve 
visibility and support faster decision -making. Advanced scrap segregation, processing, and 
melting are also helping us to improve metal recovery, product consistency, and resource 
utilization.

materials, we help conserve natural resources and reduce the energy required compared with

Iske alawa Rishabh ji ne poocha West Asia war ka impact. To hamara ye West Asia war ki 
wajah se, because scrap ki jitni bhi routing hoti hai, that is through the Suez Canal, and there 
is a lot of transshipment happening through ports inside the Strait of Hormuz. 
To iski wajah se all of a sudden there was a big shortage in the market. This led to prices going 
up very dramatically, and through the commitment of our people and our strong systems and 
processes, we were able to service our customers very well. 
We were, in fact, able to grow our market share during this time because jo doosre log service 
nahin kar paaye, woh hum log kar paaye. 
Iske alawa jo price increase hai, uski wajah se aapne comment kiya ki there should have also 
been an increased profitability. However, we want to focus on consistent profitability, jiski 
wajah se we keep ourselves completely hedged. 
So while we did increase our margin from about eleven and a half odd rupees in the last year 
to about twelve and a half rupees in the first quarter after the West Asia war, but it was largely 
driven by operational efficiency, economies of scale rather than a windfall profit because we 
are hedged.

270,000 carbon credits. 

Aside from this, in our operation as well, despite being a recycling company and always finding
