Let me begin with a brief overview of Orient Cables.
Orient Cables was incorporated in 2005 and began manufacturing in 2006, initially focusing
on land cables.
Since then, we have evolved into a diversified manufacturer of specialized cables, passive
networking equipment and customized connectivity solutions.
Our portfolio now covers networking, optical fiber, speciality power and instrumentation
cables, EDEAM radiated cable and allied products.
We serve applications across telecommunications, broadband, data centers, renewable energy,
railways, defense and other critical infrastructure sectors.
From a focused land cable manufacturer, we have progressed towards becoming an integrated
connectivity solutions provider, supported by our manufacturing and engineering capability,
global accreditations and long-term customer relationships.
We have continually invested in new processes, testing infrastructure and product development,
alongside expanding our facilities at Bihari and establishing a presence in Bangalore.
Our vision is to be a trusted partner behind critical infrastructure in India and internationally,
delivering reliable and customized solutions with speed, safety and sustainability.
What differentiates our business is the nature of our engagement with customers.
We primarily serve telecom service providers, equipment manufacturers, OEMs, system integrators,
IT solution providers and resellers.
Much of our work involves customer specific engineering and demanding technical specifications.
Typically, our engagement starts with understanding our customer's requirements, followed by
design, sampling, testing and product qualification.
Depending on this application, this process can take several months or even years.
Once qualified, products move into commercial production and recurring supply.
This technical qualification process can be a meaningful barrier to entry and makes consistency
of quality especially important.
As customers become familiar with our engineering and execution capability, these relationships
create opportunities to introduce additional products, deepen our share of customer procurement
and build a business that goes beyond supplying simply tables.
Our growth is driven by three factors.
Expansion of industries we serve, our ability to develop new and customized products and
efficient, consistent manufacturing with reliable delivery.
The combination allows us to participate in industry growth while seeking a greater share
of business from the customers we already serve.
When we look after our product portfolio, we have four broad categories.
First, networking cables and solutions, which remain our largest business.
This includes CAT5E to CAT7 cables and their variants, CCTV and coaxial cables and
terminated cable assembly.
These products serve telecom and broadband networks, data centers, enterprise networks,
surveillance and smart buildings.
Second would be speciality power and optical fiber cables and solutions.
We manufacture instrumentation, special purpose power cables, unit tube and multi-tube
optical fiber cables and fiber assembly, terminated with connectors.
These products serve high speed data transmission, telecom infrastructure, renewable energy,
industrial installation, data centers and other infrastructure applications.
Third, wire and cable harnesses, assembly and EV charging gun and cable assembly.
We entered these activities in fiscal 26 through our subsidiary OCL Green Tech Private
Limited in which orient cables holds a 51th estate.
Fourth is allied and passive networking products, including keystone jack, spatch panels,
faceplate, gang boxes, power strips and power cords.
This allows us to address more of our customers' passive networking requirements.
This diversification is deliberate.
We want to be able to support customers across multiple product requirements
other than remain dependent on any single application.
Over time, we aim to increase the contribution from specialized value added products, strengthen
cross-selling opportunities and broaden our revenue streams.
We have also entered into a 50-50 joint venture with Aditya Infotec Limited through
