year from financial perspective. On a consolidated basis, revenue increased by 
28% to crossing ₹100 crores milestone for the first time.

revenue performance was supported by healthy customer demand, improved 
execution, better capacity utilization, encouraging export traction and 
contribution from our expanded manufacturing facilities. Standalone EBITDA 
grew by 63.7% to ₹1244 lakhs with EBITDA margins improving significantly from 
10.5% to 13.3%. Most importantly, standalone profit after tax improved to ₹260
