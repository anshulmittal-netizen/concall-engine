Strategic Overview 
IRB is fundamentally an asset -led infrastructure platform, with an asset base 
of approximately ₹94,000 crore. Despite geopolitical uncertainty and 
macroeconomic headwinds, our performance has remained resilient, reflecting 
the returns from assets built steadily over the years.

This is clearly reflected in our toll revenue. Across our 100% subsidiaries, 
Private InvIT and Public InvIT, toll revenue grew around 24% year-on-year 
this quarter. This was supported by steady traffic demand and tariff 
mechanisms linked to inflation. India’s domestic growth remains our strongest 
anchor, and the Government’s continued support for Public -Private 
Partnerships gives the sector a strong runway. 
Business Update 
During the quarter, IRB InvIT Fund, our Public InvIT, took another important 
step forward. It acquired two operational highway assets from our Private 
InvIT, IRB Infrastructure Trust, with an aggregate enterprise value of ₹4,605 
crore. The transaction attr acted strong interest from leading global and 
domestic investors and raised equity capital of ₹2,000 crore into the Fund. IRB 
also participated in preferential issue by the Fund and invested ₹351 Crore. 
For the IRB Group, this is a significant step in our asset monetisation strategy. 
IRB Infrastructure Trust realises equity consideration of about ₹2,744 crore. 
This is non- dilutive capital that strengthens the Group’s balance sheet and 
financial flexibility. The transaction also adds these assets to our O&M order 
book. 
Equally important, this capital recycling positions us to pursue new 
opportunities worth about ₹8,000 crore, while maintaining balance sheet 
strength. 
The response from investors reflects the confidence we have earned through 
more than three decades of developing, operating and managing highway 
infrastructure. It also shows the growing acceptance of the InvIT structure as 
an effective way to recycle capi tal and attract long -term investment into 
infrastructure. 
The transaction benefits both InvIT platforms. It helps them scale their asset 
portfolios, strengthen cash flows and improve the potential for sustained 
distributions to investors. At the same time, it lets the Group focus on new 
growth opportunities using its established capabilities in project development 
and operations.

Operational Performance 
Our 100% subsidiaries continued to deliver strong performance. Year-on-year, 
gross toll collections on the Mumbai –Pune Expressway and the Ahmedabad–
Vadodara Expressway grew by 14% and 16% respectively, for overall growth 
of about 15% across our wholly owned portfolio. 
Our Private InvIT reported average daily toll collections of ₹13.1 crore for the 
quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared with ₹9.2 crore in the same 
quarter last year. That is about 42% year-on-year growth, on a like -for-like 
basis that excludes the 3 assets transferred to Public InvIT. The growth was 
driven by healthy traffic momentum and the addition of new assets. 
Distributions 
The strong growth in toll collections on both InvIT platforms has translated 
into significantly higher cash distributions, further strengthening IRB’s 
recurring cash flows. 
The Private InvIT declared a distribution of about ₹231 crore for Q2FY27. 
IRB’s 51% stake translates into about ₹118 crore, compared with ₹26 crore in 
Q2FY26. That is an increase of about 354% year-on-year. It was driven by 
three new revenue streams: the n ewly acquired TOT 17 and TOT 18 assets, 
and the completion of the Ganga Expressway during the year. IRB made no 
additional funding commitment for any of these. This shows our model at 
work: the InvIT platforms keep adding to the Company’s cash flows while 
improving our overall financial flexibility. 
The Public InvIT declared a distribution of about ₹269 crore for Q2FY27. With 
IRB’s effective holding of around 16%, the Company expects to receive nearly 
₹43 crore, compared with ₹32 crore in Q2FY26. That is about 35% growth 
year-on-year.

Overall, the substantial increase in distributions reflects the strong performance 
of the underlying assets, the smooth integration of newly acquired assets and 
the effectiveness of our InvIT-led monetisation strategy. 
Dividend and Order Book 
In line with our dividend distribution policy, the Company has declared an 
interim dividend of about ₹60 crore for Q2FY27. 
Our total order book stands at about ₹43,400 crore, including an EPC order 
book of ~ ₹1,200 crore. 
Outlook 
Looking ahead, we believe this capital -light approach will be an important 
driver of our next phase of growth. It further establishes IRB as a leading 
infrastructure sponsor and O&M operator. Building on our ₹94,000 crore asset 
base, we remain firmly on track to reach a ₹1,40,000 crore asset base by 2030, 
creating sustainable long-term value for all our stakeholders.
